# Trace fixtures

Four GPX traces, and the ground truth that makes them testable. Generated by
`tools/fixtures/gen-traces.js` from a seeded PRNG, so regenerating produces byte-identical
files:

```
node tools/fixtures/gen-traces.js core/location/src/test/resources/traces
```

The script prints the ground-truth figures on every run. If a fixture is ever changed on
purpose, take the new numbers from that output and update both this file and `Traces.kt` —
never reverse-engineer them back out of the GPX.

All four are around Addis Ababa (8.9806 N, 38.7578 E), walked at 1.4 m/s, with Gaussian
position jitter of σ = 1.5 m per axis and a reported accuracy of 4.0 m. Accuracy is written
into a non-standard `<accuracy>` element inside `<extensions>`; GPX has no accuracy field, and
deriving it from `<hdop>` would make every fixture depend on a fudge factor instead of a stated
number.

## The fixtures

| File | Points | Interval | What it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `clean_walk_1km.gpx` | 715 | 1 s | The distance-accuracy bar |
| `walk_1km_with_outliers.gpx` | 715 | 1 s | The outlier-rejection bar |
| `stationary_30min.gpx` | 600 | 3 s | The step gate |
| `walk_with_5min_gap.gpx` | 240 | 1 s | Segment breaks and Kalman reset |

### `clean_walk_1km.gpx`

A gently curving walk. Noise-free path length **999.60 m** — the figure the filter chain has to
recover to within 2% (`BUILD_PLAN.md` phase 4).

### `walk_1km_with_outliers.gpx`

Identical to the clean walk, same PRNG seed, with six points displaced 200 m north/south at
indices **97, 198, 301, 404, 512, 631**. Never adjacent, so no outlier is ever judged against
another as its reference.

Their reported accuracy is unchanged at 4.0 m, which is the point: an accuracy-only gate would
pass every one of them. They have to be caught on implied speed — 200 m in one second.

### `stationary_30min.gpx`

A device that never moved, for half an hour.

### `walk_with_5min_gap.gpx`

Two 120-point walks of 166.60 m each, separated by a 300 s gap in the timestamps, **inside a
single `<trkseg>`**. The break can therefore only be inferred from the times, not from the file
structure — which is the whole point, since a real gap from a tunnel or an OEM killing the
service does not helpfully close a segment tag.

The device is **420 m** away when it resumes. That distance was never observed and must not be
counted.

## Ground truth vs. measured

Measured through `ReplayLocationEngine` → `FixPipeline` with a default `TrackingConfig`. The raw
column is the naive sum of consecutive **raw** fixes, and it is the reason all of this exists.

| Trace | Raw sum | Truth | Filtered | Error |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `clean_walk_1km` | 2011.28 m | 999.60 m | 1012.15 m | +1.26% |
| `walk_1km_with_outliers` | 4371.52 m | 999.60 m | 1011.89 m | +1.23% |
| `stationary_30min` | 1652.15 m | 0 m | 14.54 m | — |
| `walk_with_5min_gap` | 1106.72 m | 333.20 m | 331.79 m | −0.42% |

Three things worth reading off that table:

1. **Jitter roughly doubles a raw distance sum.** 2011 m for a 1 km walk. A chain that passed
   values through unfiltered would miss the 2% bar by a hundred percentage points.
2. **The six outliers add 2.36 km to the raw sum and 0.26 m to the filtered one.** The clean and
   outlier traces land 0.026% apart, which is what "rejected without displacing the line" means
   in numbers.
3. **A stationary device sums to 1.65 km raw and 14.5 m filtered.** `LOCATION_ENGINE.md` calls
   the step gate "the difference between credible and useless distance figures"; this is that
   difference, measured.

The clean-walk margin is 1.26% against a 2% bar. That is real headroom but not vast, and most of
it is the Kalman filter cutting the corners of the curve — a fixture with sharper turns would sit
closer to the limit. Worth remembering before tightening the bar.
